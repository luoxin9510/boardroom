## 触发
当你占着先手出手攻击，掷骰。

## Trigger
When you have the initiative and attack, roll.

| 结果 | 中文 | English | 后果 |
|---|---|---|---|
| strong | 打中了，还能接着打。 | A hit, and you keep the pressure. | progress×harm & initiative+ |
| weak | 打中了，但失了先手。 | A hit, but you lose the initiative. | progress×harm & initiative- |
| miss | 落空，对手反击。 | A miss; they strike back. | initiative- & move:toll |
