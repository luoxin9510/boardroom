## 触发
当你挨了一下，先扣生命，然后掷生命或钢铁里较高的那个。

## Trigger
When you take a blow, lose health first, then roll the higher of health or iron.

| weak | 你咬牙继续。 | You grit your teeth. | |
| miss | 情况很糟。 | It goes badly. | momentum-1 & 二选一(mark:wounded \| oracle:last-stand) |
