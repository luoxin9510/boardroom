## 触发
当你在野外歇一歇，掷补给。

## Trigger
When you stop to rest in the wild, roll supply.

| 结果 | 中文 | English | 后果 |
|---|---|---|---|
| strong | 选两项。 | Choose two. | 选2(health+1 \| spirit+1 \| momentum+1 \| supply+1) |
| weak | 选一项。 | Choose one. | 选1(health+1 \| spirit+1 \| momentum+1 \| supply+1) |
| miss | 一夜无眠。 | A sleepless night. | 可多选(spirit-1 \| supply-1) |
