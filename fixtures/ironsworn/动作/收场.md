## 触发
当你想结束这场战斗，做一次进度掷骰。

## Trigger
When you try to end the fight, make a progress roll.

| 结果 | 中文 | English | 后果 |
|---|---|---|---|
| strong | 战斗结束。 | The fight is over. | |
| weak | 结束了，但有代价。 | Over, at a cost. | 二选一(health-1 \| supply-1) |
| miss | 局势失控。 | It gets out of hand. | move:toll |
