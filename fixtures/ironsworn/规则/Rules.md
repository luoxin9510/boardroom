A short summary for the test content (original, not the rulebook). Import your own content pack to read the full rules here.

## Action roll
<!-- topic: action-roll -->

d6 + stat + adds (max 10) against two d10: beat both for a strong hit, one for a weak hit, none is a miss. Matching d10s mean a twist.

## Momentum
<!-- topic: momentum -->

Rises with success, falls with failure; burn it to cancel challenge dice below it.

## Progress
<!-- topic: progress -->

Ten boxes; higher ranks mark less each time; a progress roll counts filled boxes.

## Debilities
<!-- topic: debilities -->

Wounded, shaken and the rest lower your maximum momentum.

## Vows
<!-- topic: vows -->

Promise to start a vow, step it forward, then keep it.

## Combat
<!-- topic: combat -->

Engage for the initiative, hit to mark the foe's track, finish to end the fight.

## Journey
<!-- topic: journey -->

The test content has no journey moves.

## Oracles
<!-- topic: oracles -->

Ask yes or no, or roll a table for inspiration.

## Experience
<!-- topic: experience -->

Keep promises to earn experience; spend it on assets and upgrades.
