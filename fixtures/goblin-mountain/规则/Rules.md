A short summary for the test cards (written for this app, not the original rulebook). Import your own content pack to read the full rules from your vault here.

## Goal
<!-- topic: goal -->

Get your four adventurers (dice) through the tunnel to the village, collecting Fame, Treasure and villagers. You need at least one die in the village to win.

## Moving
<!-- topic: move -->

Each turn move one die, from the entrance or the tunnel, to a location further than your leading die. Meet the location's colour and strength requirement; occupied locations are off limits.

### Left behind
<!-- topic: behind -->

When you pick up a die from the tunnel, every die behind it (including those at the entrance) loses 1. A die at 0 is caught.

## Location actions
<!-- topic: actions -->

After placing, you may use the location: pay the cost on the left, take the reward in the box. Repeatable locations can be used as long as you can pay. Treasure is capped at 8.

## Fate
<!-- topic: fate -->

Before moving you may spend Fate: swap two dice, ±1 on one, set one to 4, or reset all Stealth dice to 1.

## Solo rules
<!-- topic: solo -->

All dice start at 3; once before your first move, one die −1 and another +1.

### Stealth dice
<!-- topic: stealth -->

After each move, roll the Stealth die matching the die you moved.

### Goblin King
<!-- topic: king -->

The Goblin King moves 1 plus one per 5 or 6 among the Stealth dice (4s count too once your leading die reaches the special card). Where he stops on your dice they lose 1, and 1 more per remaining step; he moves on once they are caught.

### Village
<!-- topic: village -->

Appears once the Goblin King leaves the Warrens. The first four dice in earn bonus Fame if strong enough.

## Scoring
<!-- topic: scoring -->

Fame + 1 per 2 Treasure + (villagers − 2) − 2 per caught die.
